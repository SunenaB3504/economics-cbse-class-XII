# Gap Analysis: Unit 7 - Current Challenges Facing Indian Economy

This report identifies the discrepancies between the current `topics.ts` / `cheatSheet.ts` content and the source NCERT textbook materials (`keec104` to `keec107`).

## 1. Chapter 4: Human Capital Formation in India (HCF)

### 🔴 Missing Technical Details
- **Distinction Matrix**: The text provides a clear comparison between **Physical Capital** and **Human Capital** across 6 bases (Tangibility, Mobility, Separability, Formation, Depreciation, Nature of Benefits). The current code only covers "HCF vs Human Development".
- **Regulatory Bodies**: While the names are present, their specific roles (e.g., NCERT facilitating schools, UGC facilitating universities) are missing.
- **Investment Rationale**: The "Mandatory State Intervention" section lacks the "Information Asymmetry" and "Externalities" terminology used in the text.

### 📊 Missing Statistics & Committees
- **Tapas Majumdar Committee (1999)**: The estimate of **Rs 1.37 lakh crore** investment needed over 10 years.
- **Education Cess**: The specific **2% levy** on union taxes for elementary education.
- **Expenditure Trends**: 1952 vs 2014 comparisons (7.92% -> 15.7% of govt spending; 0.64% -> 4.13% of GDP).
- **Literacy Rates**: 2017-18 specific stats (Male 82%, Female 66%).

## 2. Chapter 5: Rural Development

### 🔴 Missing Concepts
- **Kudumbashree**: The Kerala-based women's poverty reduction programme (acclaimed as the largest informal bank in Asia).
- **Tanwa Project**: Tamil Nadu Women in Agriculture (training in vermicompost/organic farming).
- **Alternate Marketing Examples**: Specific names like **Apni Mandi** (Punjab/HR/RJ), **Hadaspar Mandi** (Pune), **Rythu Bazars** (AP/Telangana), and **Uzhavar Sandies** (TN).
- **SAGY (2014)**: Saansad Adarsh Gram Yojana details.

### 📊 Missing Statistics
- **SHG Scale**: 6 crore women in 54 lakh SHGs (as of May 2019).
- **Jan-Dhan Scale**: 50 crore accounts milestone.
- **Livestock Share**: Poultry (61%), Cattle & Buffaloes (22%).
- **Fisheries**: Inland (65%) vs Marine (35%) contribution to value.

## 3. Chapter 6: Employment

### 🔴 Missing Concepts
- **"Worker" Definition**: The text includes those temporarily absent (illness/festivals) and "helpers" in the definition.
- **Workforce Vulnerability**: The concept that people in India cannot remain unemployed for long due to poverty, leading them to accept "unpleasant/dangerous" jobs.
- **Informalisation Case Study**: The **Ahmedabad Textile Mills** closure (1,50,000 workers losing security) providing a real-world context for informalisation.
- **Jobless Growth**: While mentioned, the specific comparison of GDP growth (>5%) vs employment growth (<2%) since the 1950s is not explicit.

### 📊 Missing Statistics
- **Workforce Size**: **545 million** strong workforce (2022-23).
- **Participation Rates**: Rural (42%) vs Urban (38%).
- **Formal vs Informal**: The Stark **6% vs 94%** distribution.

## 4. Chapter 7: Environment & Sustainable Development

### 🔴 Missing Technical Details
- **Environment Functions**: The "Aesthetic Services" (scenery) function is usually ignored.
- **Daly's 5 Rules**: Herman Daly's specific criteria for achieving sustainability (Population limit, Input efficiency, Extraction vs Regeneration rate, etc.).
- **Specific Movements**: The **Appiko Movement** (Karnataka, 1983) as the southern counterpart to Chipko.
- **Regulatory Scale**: CPCB's **17 categories** of highly polluting industries.

### 📊 Missing Statistics
- **Forest Land**: Per capita forest land (0.06 hectare) vs requirement (0.47).
- **Soil Erosion**: 5.3 billion tonnes/year.
- **Iron-ore**: India holding 8% of world reserves.
- **Vehicle Pollution**: Growth from 3 lakh (1951) to 35 crore (2022).

The Unit 7 Theory should be expanded into a structured repository of these case studies and statistics to match the high-yield requirement for CBSE exams, where specific committee names and percentages are often asked in 1-mark or 3-mark questions.
