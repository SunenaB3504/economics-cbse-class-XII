Economics (030) Master Cheat Sheet

Exam Morning Rapid Review Matrix & SQP Tricks

🔥 CRITICAL UNITS (> 12 Marks Avg in SQPs)

Statistically the highest yielding units. Review these first. Ensure formulas and macro models are perfect.

Unit 6: Indian Economic Development (1947-90) & Reforms 14.25 Marks

  • Chronology Trap: The Great Leap Forward violently began in China (1958) drastically before Economic Reforms formally started (China 1978, Pakistan 1988, India 1991).
  • IPR 1956 Rule: It classified industries into three schedules relying strictly on the State to lead industrialization.
  • LPG (Globalisation): Integration of domestic economy with the world economy.

Unit 7: Current Challenges Facing Indian Economy 13.75 Marks

  • Informalisation Trap: Vigorously spot this keyword if you see labour drifting away from formal structured factories heavily into absolute informal wage labor without secure social protections.
  • HCF vs HD: Human Capital treats human beings as a *means* to an end (productivity). Human Development treats humans as an *end* themselves (better life).
  • MGNREGA ensures 100 days of guaranteed wage employment.

Unit 1: National Income & Related Aggregates 12.75 Marks

  • The T/F First Rule (Assertion-Reasoning): Instantly verify if the Assertion statement is factually True entirely on its own.
  • The Fixed Asset Rule: If it is measured "over a period of time" (Investment, Income), it is a Flow. "At a specific point" (Wealth, Money Supply) is a Stock.
  • Formulas: NNPFC = National Income. GNPMP = GDPMP + NFIA.

Unit 3: Determination of Income and Employment 12.00 Marks

  • The "Leakage" Rule (Inverse Relation Trap): Higher Marginal Propensity to Save (MPS) drastically means a Lower Investment Multiplier (k = 1 / MPS).
  • Deficient Demand: Handled by decreasing taxes and bank rate, or increasing Govt spending.
  • Equilibrium: AD = AS, or S = I.

🟡 HIGH-YIELD UNITS (8 - 11 Marks Avg in SQPs)

Unit 2: Money & Banking 10.25 Marks

  • The "Leakage" Rule (Credit Creation): Higher Legal Reserve Ratio (LRR) logically means Lower Credit Creation (Multiplier = 1 / LRR).
  • OMO Trap: RBI sells bonds to absorb liquidity (Decrease MS). RBI buys bonds to inject liquidity (Increase MS).
  • CRR & SLR both belong to the Legal Reserve Ratio (LRR).

Unit 4: Government Budget & The Economy 9.00 Marks

  • The "Sins of the Past" Rule (Primary Deficit Trap): It purely measures "current year overspending". Always aggressively subtract past debt interest: Primary Deficit = Fiscal Deficit - Interest Payments.
  • Fiscal Deficit Formula Trap: `Revenue Deficit + Capital Expenditure - Non-debt Capital Receipts`.
  • Revenue vs Capital: Revenue receipts do NOT create a liability or reduce assets.

🟢 QUICK REVIEW UNITS (< 6 Marks Avg in SQPs)

These units carry lower mathematical weightage but are highly scoring if conceptual rules are remembered.

Unit 5: Balance of Payments & Forex 5.50 Marks

  • The "Apple" Rule (Currency Decoding Trap): If ₹82 = $1 becomes ₹83 = $1, you are paying *more* Rupees per Dollar. The Rupee has Depreciated, directly promoting Indian Exports!
  • Accommodating vs Autonomous items: Autonomous = for profit. Accommodating = strictly to cover BoP imbalances.

Unit 8: Development Experience of India (Comparison) 4.50 Marks

  • The "Impatient Leap" Rule (Sectoral Contribution Trap): India and Pakistan dangerously leapfrogged directly into the service sector, skipping the heavy industrialization route that China classically followed.
  • Demographic Trap: China has the lowest birth rate and population growth (One Child Policy), but Pakistan has the highest growth rate.
  • HDI: Human Development Index combines Life Expectancy, Education, and GNI per capita.
